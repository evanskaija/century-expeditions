Overview

Discover the Beauty
of Mikumi

Explore broad grasslands, woodland and scenic horizons on a safari through one of southern Tanzania's rewarding wildlife destinations.

Mikumi offers an inviting setting for game drives, with changing landscapes and opportunities to discover both iconic wildlife and the smaller wonders of the bush.

Read more about Mikumi

Take time to watch animals moving across the plains, explore woodland habitats with your guide and pause for mountain-framed views. Our team can shape a relaxed Mikumi stay around your interests or combine it with other southern Tanzania destinations.

Landscape and Wildlife

Wildlife

Life Across the Open Plains

Look for elephants, giraffes, zebras, buffalo and lions as your guide explores the park's diverse habitats.

Birdlife adds colour to the landscape, while waterholes can offer rewarding opportunities to observe animals gathering and interacting.

Read more about wildlife viewing

Follow the day's natural opportunities with your guide: watch the grasslands, look closely around woodland edges and pause near water sources. Wildlife sightings vary, so allow time to enjoy the scenery and the unexpected encounters along the way.
