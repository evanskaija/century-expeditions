Volunteer Planning, Made Clear

What is included in program fees?

Inclusions vary by project. Request a written breakdown of program support, accommodation, meals and transfers, along with any additional costs, before confirming your plans.

What preparation or screening is required?

Requirements depend on the role and project. Our team will explain relevant experience, preparation and screening, including any age restrictions, before a placement is confirmed.

Can I combine volunteering with a safari?

Yes, we can discuss a safari or beach stay around your project dates. Project commitments and availability will guide the itinerary.
