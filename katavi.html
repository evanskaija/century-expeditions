Overview

A Journey Into the Wild

Discover an expansive landscape of seasonal floodplains, woodland and waterways in western Tanzania.

Katavi invites adventurous travellers to experience the quieter rhythms of the bush, with remarkable scenery and wildlife encounters far from busy safari routes.

Read more about Katavi

Allow time to explore at an unhurried pace. The landscape changes with seasonal water levels, bringing different opportunities along the floodplains and woodland edges. Our team can help you choose a camp and itinerary around your interests.

Katavi Gallery

Wildlife

Wildlife Around the Water

Look for buffalo, elephants, hippos and diverse birdlife as your guide explores the park's changing habitats.

Seasonal water levels shape the landscape and influence where animals gather. Lions and other predators add to the possibilities of every game drive.

Discover Katavi's wildlife rhythm

Watch the water's edge, pause for birdlife and follow the day's opportunities with your guide. Each visit unfolds differently as animals move through the changing landscape. Time and patience make space for memorable encounters.
