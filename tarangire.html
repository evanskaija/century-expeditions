Overview

A Vital Water Source

Located in northern Tanzania, Tarangire is a rewarding destination for wildlife enthusiasts, where ancient baobabs rise above golden grasslands.

Named after the Tarangire River, the park offers varied landscapes and a vital water source for the animals that call it home. Follow the river through woodland and open savannah for a safari full of discovery.

Read more about Tarangire

During the dry season, the river draws wildlife from the surrounding landscape. Explore riverbanks, seasonal wetlands and baobab-dotted plains with a guide who knows where to look. Pair Tarangire with Ngorongoro and Serengeti for a varied northern Tanzania itinerary.

Wildlife

Large Herds of Elephants

Discover impressive elephant herds alongside lions, leopards, buffalo and a rich variety of other wildlife.

Birdlife adds colour to every safari. During the dry season, animals gather around the Tarangire River, creating rewarding opportunities for game viewing.

Discover the wildlife experience

Watch elephants move between woodland and water, look for predators resting in the shade, and take time to appreciate the park's smaller residents. Each game drive follows the rhythms of the landscape, with wildlife encounters unfolding naturally.
