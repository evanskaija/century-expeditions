Family Travel, Made Easier

Answers to a few common questions from families.

Can the itinerary suit different ages?

Yes. Tell us the ages of your children and everyone's interests. We can suggest a pace, stays and activities that suit your family, taking individual activity age requirements into account.

Can we combine safari and Zanzibar?

Absolutely. Combine wildlife experiences on the mainland with time beside the Indian Ocean. Our team can coordinate travel connections and beach stays around your dates.

Can you help with rooms and dietary needs?

Tell us your room preferences and dietary or accessibility needs. We will discuss suitable properties and confirm arrangements with providers before booking.
