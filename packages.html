Frequently Asked Questions

What is included in the price?

The existing listings supply starting prices but do not specify their inclusion basis. Request a written quotation confirming accommodation, meals, activities, transport, fees, exclusions and price conditions before making a booking.

Can I customise a listed itinerary?

Yes. Select a package and share your preferences, or choose Custom Journey to tell us your destinations and duration.

How is my booking confirmed?

Sending this form creates an enquiry. Our reservations team reviews your preferences and prepares a proposal. Your booking is confirmed only after the agreed booking process is completed and written confirmation is issued.
