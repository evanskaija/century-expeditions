Overview

A Wilderness
Worth Exploring

Discover a dramatic landscape of rolling hills, ancient baobabs and open woodland, shaped by the Great Ruaha River.

Ruaha invites you to slow down and explore the quieter rhythms of the bush, with varied habitats and memorable wildlife encounters.

Read more about Ruaha

Follow rocky riverbanks and woodland tracks, pause beneath the baobabs and take in the park's changing scenery. Our guides help you discover the larger wildlife and the smaller details that make an unhurried safari rewarding.

Landscape and Wildlife

Wildlife

Wildlife in Its Element

Look for elephants, lions, giraffes, buffalo and a rich variety of birdlife as you explore Ruaha's changing landscapes.

Seasonal water sources influence where animals gather, while experienced guides help you discover both the iconic species and the smaller details of the wilderness.

Discover the wildlife experience

Take time to watch the riverbanks, follow fresh tracks with your guide and enjoy the park's quieter corners. Each day unfolds differently as wildlife moves between water, grassland and woodland.
