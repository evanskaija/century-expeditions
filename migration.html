Migration Planning, Made Clear

Can river crossings be guaranteed?

No. Crossings are unpredictable and depend on natural conditions and herd behaviour. We plan suitable regions and allow time to explore, while keeping expectations realistic.

Which safari region suits our travel dates?

The best region changes with the seasons and rainfall. Share your dates and interests so our team can suggest camp locations and a route suited to the expected herd movements.

Can I combine migration viewing with Zanzibar?

Yes. Add a relaxed island stay after your safari. We can coordinate suitable travel connections, beach accommodation and time for both experiences.
